Yeh Din Kyun

Gen Z Din Kyun Kehte Hain?

Ismein "Gen Z" isliye kaha jaata hai kyunki ye protest mukhya roop se college students aur bahut kam umar ke unemployed youth ne khada kiya — bina kisi bade political party ke bainer ke. Movement ka naam pada "Cockroach Janta Party" (CJP) — ek satire jo Supreme Court ke Chief Justice ke ek bayaan se nikla, jismein unhone bekaar/unemployed youth ko "cockroaches aur parasites" bola tha jo social media activism ke zariye institutions par attack karte hain. Youth ne usi shabd ko apna symbol bana liya.

Protest ka trigger tha 2026 ka NEET paper leak aur CBSE on-screen marking mein gadbadi — lekin iske peeche asli gussa tha job scarcity, mehengi education, aur exam system mein bharosa khona.
